Unofficial Intellivision core for CollectorVision Phoenix - 0101
Should be compatible with both releases of hardware.

YOU MUST PROVIDE INTELLIVISION ROMS. Either in the root of the SD card, or in a folder named "INTV":

+----------------------------------------------------------+
¦            ROM            ¦ Size ¦  CRC-32  ¦ Needed for ¦
+---------------------------+------+----------+------------¦
¦ EXEC                      ¦ 8K   ¦ CBCE86F7 ¦ everything ¦    exec.bin
+---------------------------+------+----------+------------¦
¦ GROM                      ¦ 2K   ¦ 683A4158 ¦ everything ¦    grom.bin
+---------------------------+------+----------+------------¦
¦ ECS                       ¦ 24K  ¦ EA790A06 ¦ ECS games  ¦    ecs.bin
+---------------------------+------+----------+------------¦
¦ Intellivoice (SP0256-012) ¦ 2K   ¦ 0DE7579D ¦ speech     ¦    sp0256-012.bin
+----------------------------------------------------------+

- They will be matched by CRC so should work even if the filenames differ.
- Other versions: a different EXEC or GROM version is still used if it's named EXEC.BIN or GROM.BIN.
- Missing files:
- No EXEC or GROM: starting a game shows "NO EXEC/GROM IN /INTV".
- No ECS ROM: the ECS stays off.
- No voice ROM: the Intellivoice is switched off. 

Supports BIN, INT, ITV raw images, and optional CFG files. Max size is 250k words.

At the base of the menu you can toggle controller ports, as well as ECS and JLP autodetection.

Note that JLP is not well tested. Saving is not supported.

The top action buttons are left and right on the Coleco pad, and both will trigger the lower right action button.
Hold RESET for 4 seconds to return to file menu.
