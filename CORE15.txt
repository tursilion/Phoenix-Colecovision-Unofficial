Unofficial Joust Core for CollectorVision Phoenix
Should be compatible with both releases of hardware.

You will need to provide Joust ROMs, in a folder on the SD card named JOUST. Make sure to unzip them into the folder.

'5' key or SELECT will add a credit. '1' or START for a 1 player game. '2' for a two player game. For Coleco pads you will use left  and right to move and either fire button to flap.
